Everyday habits for healthy teeth

Simple routines that protect your smile between dental visits.

Brush twice a dayUse a fluoride toothpaste and brush for two minutes.
Clean between your teethFloss or use interdental brushes once a day.
Go easy on sugarLimit sugary snacks and fizzy drinks, especially between meals.
Replace your toothbrushChange it every three months or when the bristles wear out.
Do not ignore painTooth pain or bleeding gums are a reason to see a dentist.
